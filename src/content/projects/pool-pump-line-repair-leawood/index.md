## Why the box is at grade

The National Electrical Code requires a junction box to stay accessible: you must be able to reach the wiring without digging it up. A splice buried in the dirt with no way to get to it would not pass.

The homeowners also asked us to place the box where they can open it again in the future. If they add landscape lighting, the box gives them a ready connection point in the yard without trenching all the way back to the house.
